Room preview

Mouse
Drag on an empty lane → new Hold clip · drag a clip → move · drag its edges → resize · click a clip → edit.
Snaps to 100 ms (hold Alt for free). Overlaps are refused (red).
Rows: click a label · Shift = range · Ctrl = toggle · click a wall name = whole wall.
Click the ruler to move the playhead. Ctrl+wheel = zoom.
Keys: Del delete · Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy / paste at playhead · Ctrl+D duplicate right after the selected clip(s) (repeat to build a sequence; with one clip selected, rows you selected get the copy too) · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo · Space play / pause.
Stagger: select rows (or clips) that have a clip at the playhead; the first keeps its time, each next frame starts offset later.
Each frame can hold at most 24 steps (gaps count). Rows over the limit turn red; Run mode will not play that show.
